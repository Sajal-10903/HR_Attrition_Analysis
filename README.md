# HR Employee Attrition & Retention Analysis

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze employee attrition patterns and identify key factors that influence employee retention. The analysis aims to support HR teams in understanding why employees leave and which groups are at higher risk of attrition.

---

## Dataset
The dataset contains employee-level information related to demographics, job roles, compensation, workload, and satisfaction levels.

### Key Attributes Used
- Age
- Department
- Job Role
- Gender
- Monthly Income
- Years at Company
- OverTime
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Attrition (Yes/No)

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and column selection to focus on attrition-related factors  
- Conversion of attrition labels into numerical format for analysis  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to evaluate attrition patterns across:
  - Departments
  - Overtime status
  - Income levels
  - Employee experience
  - Job satisfaction and work-life balance  
- Visual analysis to highlight key trends and risk factors

---

## Key Insights
- Attrition is not uniform across departments; certain departments experience higher employee turnover.
- Employees working overtime show significantly higher attrition rates, indicating workload stress as a major contributor.
- Employees with lower average income and fewer years at the company are more likely to leave.
- Lower job satisfaction and poorer work-life balance are associated with increased attrition.

---

## Business Implications
- HR teams can reduce attrition by addressing workload issues, especially for employees working overtime.
- Department-specific retention strategies can be more effective than generalized HR policies.
- Improving employee satisfaction and work-life balance can positively impact retention.

---

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how HR data can be analyzed to uncover meaningful attrition patterns and support data-driven employee retention strategies. The insights generated can help organizations proactively address workforce challenges and improve long-term employee engagement.
